# "La règle du six-seven": 1-minute video prompt (9:16)

Current video AIs make clips of about 8 to 10 s. For a 1-minute video:
- **Option A**: a tool that takes a timed storyboard (Sora storyboard, Kling multi-shot): paste the **master prompt** (section 1).
- **Option B**: Veo 3 / Kling / Runway: generate the **6 clips of 10 s** (section 2) one at a time, then put them end to end in CapCut. Each clip repeats the character descriptions so they stay identical.

Dialogue is in French in quotes (Veo 3 and Sora generate the voices); the description is in English (better followed).

---

## 1. Master prompt (60 s, timed)
```
Vertical 9:16, 60-second 3D animated short film, Pixar-like style, photorealistic textures, bright French middle-school classroom, cinematic lighting, French dialogue, energetic comedy pacing.
CHARACTERS (keep identical in every shot): NOAH, a 12-year-old boy with messy brown hair, big expressive eyes and a blue hoodie. M. DURAND, a tired 60-year-old math teacher, bald on top with grey hair on the sides, round glasses, thick grey mustache, brown tweed jacket with elbow patches. LA DIRECTRICE, an elegant 50-year-old principal with a short silver bob, red glasses and a navy suit. THE CLASS, 25 diverse 12-year-old students. THE 6-7 GESTURE: both hands raised palms up at chest height, alternately moving up and down like a weighing scale.

[0:00-0:10] Close-up on NOAH at his desk, worried. Deep male narrator: "Dans la classe de Noah, il y a un problème. Il s'appelle… le six-seven." Cut to the whole class jumping up doing THE 6-7 GESTURE, shouting "SIX SEVEN !", papers flying.
[0:10-0:20] M. DURAND at the blackboard, exhausted, opening a textbook: "Ouvrez vos livres page soixante…" The class: "…SEPT ! SIX SEVEN !" He slams his desk, furious: "Ça suffit ! Le prochain qui dit six-seven copie six cent soixante-sept fois !"
[0:20-0:30] Tense silence, students with puffed cheeks holding their breath. M. DURAND hands back tests with a big red 6 and a big red 7: "Lucas… six. Emma… sept." Students shake, hands under their desks, nobody laughs.
[0:30-0:40] The classroom door opens, LA DIRECTRICE enters smiling, holding a paper: "Monsieur Durand, félicitations ! Votre classe a la meilleure moyenne du collège." M. DURAND: "Ah oui ? Combien ?" She shows the paper reading "6,7": "Six… virgule sept."
[0:40-0:50] Extreme close-up of M. DURAND, eyes widening, mustache twitching, total silence. Suddenly he raises his hands and does THE 6-7 GESTURE with all his energy: "SIX… SEVEN !" The whole class explodes: "SIX SEVEN ! SIX SEVEN !", papers flying like confetti, camera shake.
[0:50-1:00] At dusk, M. DURAND alone in the empty classroom writing lines on the blackboard, a huge pile of copied pages on his desk. Narrator: "Et c'est comme ça que Monsieur Durand a copié six cent soixante-sept fois : je ne dirai plus six-seven." He sighs, then a tiny smile and a discreet 6-7 gesture.
No subtitles in the image, no watermark.
```

## 3. Assembly (CapCut)
1. Put the 6 clips end to end; cut the dead time between clips.
2. Captions: one word at a time, white UPPERCASE, black outline; every "SIX SEVEN !" in big yellow that shakes, with a bass "boom".
3. Music: light comic beat, cut during the silences (clips 3 and 5), big drop on the teacher's "SIX SEVEN !".
4. If a character changes look between two clips: regenerate that clip, using an image of the previous clip as the starting image (image-to-video).
5. TikTok: label it **AI-generated content**; tags `#sixseven #67 #ecole #prof #humour`.
