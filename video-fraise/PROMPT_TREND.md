# "Fruit drama" trend: complete prompt pack (French TikTok, 9:16, ~60 s)

Original characters, not taken from @onlyfrubidi_off. Image and video prompts are in English because generators follow them better; the voice and subtitles are in French.

## 1. The trend formula (why it works)
1. **Hook in 2 s**: a strong image plus a punchy first sentence.
2. **Injustice**: a kind, poor character exploited by a rich, nasty one.
3. **Secret**: the kind one does something admirable in hiding.
4. **Betrayal**: the nasty one steals or lies.
5. **Public reveal**: a detail exposes the liar in front of everyone.
6. **Karma twist**: the roles flip, with a comic final line.
- 12 to 16 shots of 3 to 5 s, a cut on every sentence, a voice-over plus 3 character voices, one-word captions.

## 2. Master style (paste at the end of EVERY image prompt)
```
3D animated movie still, Pixar-like character design, photorealistic textures, glossy fruit skin with visible pores and seeds, cinematic lighting, warm rim light, shallow depth of field, rich colors, highly detailed, vertical 9:16, no text, no watermark
```

## 3. Characters (always paste them word for word, to keep them identical)
- **FRAISE** (heroine): `a young woman whose head is a glossy red strawberry with golden seeds and fresh green leaves on top, big expressive brown eyes, small mouth, slim human body, white shirt and pink baker apron`
- **MADAME CITRON** (villain): `a haughty woman whose head is a bright yellow lemon with dimpled skin, thin arched eyebrows, smug smile, white pastry-chef jacket with black buttons and a tall white chef hat`
- **MONSIEUR KIWI** (judge): `a stern older man whose head is a fuzzy brown kiwi fruit, round gold glasses, black suit, white shirt and red bow tie`
- **Extras**: `audience of people with fruit heads (apple, orange, grape bunch, blueberry), elegant clothes`

> Tip: generate a "character sheet" for each character first (front view, plain background), then use it as a reference image (ChatGPT "use this character", Midjourney `--cref`, Leonardo "Character Reference") for every scene.

## 5. French voice-over (≈ 50 s), with a voice per character
- **NARRATEUR**: deep male, lively storyteller tone. ElevenLabs: stability 35 %, style 40 %, speed 1.1.
- **MADAME CITRON**: dry, haughty female. Stability 30 %, style 60 %.
- **FRAISE**: young, soft female. Stability 45 %, style 30 %.
- **MONSIEUR KIWI**: severe, slow male. Stability 55 %, style 25 %.

```
NARRATEUR : Voici Fraise. Elle travaille chez Madame Citron, la pâtisserie la plus chic de la ville.
NARRATEUR : Elle arrive à cinq heures. Elle pétrit. Elle cuit. Elle décore. Et elle gagne… trois euros.
MADAME CITRON : Tu es lente, Fraise. Et tes gâteaux sont moches.
NARRATEUR : Fraise ne répond rien. Elle baisse les yeux.
NARRATEUR : Mais la nuit, en secret, Fraise imagine un gâteau. Le plus beau du monde.
NARRATEUR : Un soir, Madame Citron le découvre.
MADAME CITRON : Ce gâteau-là, c'est moi qui l'ai fait. Compris ?
NARRATEUR : Le lendemain, c'est le grand concours. Le juge ? Monsieur Kiwi. Le plus sévère du pays.
NARRATEUR : Madame Citron présente le gâteau de Fraise. Comme si c'était le sien.
MONSIEUR KIWI : Magnifique. Quel est l'ingrédient secret ?
NARRATEUR : Madame Citron devient toute pâle.
MADAME CITRON : Euh… du sucre ?
MONSIEUR KIWI : Mauvaise réponse.
NARRATEUR : Alors, du fond de la salle, une petite voix répond.
FRAISE : De la fleur d'oranger. Et une pincée de cannelle.
MONSIEUR KIWI : Exact. Alors c'est toi, la vraie pâtissière.
NARRATEUR : Ce jour-là, Fraise a gagné le concours. Et Madame Citron ? Elle fait la vaisselle… chez Fraise.
```

## 6. Editing (CapCut)
- Captions: **one word at a time**, white **UPPERCASE**, heavy font (Montserrat ExtraBold or "Classic" in CapCut), black outline, centred a little below the middle. Key words in yellow.
- A cut on every sentence; slight zoom-in (105 → 110 %) on each shot.
- Music: soft piano or pizzicato, tension at the contest, triumphant at the end, about −18 dB under the voice.
- Sound effects: "ding" on 5:00, coins on "trois euros", buzzer on "Mauvaise réponse", applause at the end.
- TikTok: label it **AI-generated content**.
